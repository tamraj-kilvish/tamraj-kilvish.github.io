How It Works

1

Create Tags

Tag is like a category for your expenses. See overall total and month-wise expense breakdown in the tag view.

2

Share with Others

Share tags with family or friends who can view expenses and attach their own transactions to the tag.

3

Import from UPI

Import expenses from your UPI app. Kilvish automatically extracts information using OCR technology.

4

Get Notified

Receive instant notifications when someone adds or updates expenses to shared tags.

Why Kilvish?

Track Expense Across People

Perfect for families! Create a household tag to track monthly expenses when multiple family members are spending. Everyone stays informed about total household spending.

Track Recurring Payments

Pay a vendor regularly? Import and tag all payments to keep them organized in one place. Share the tag with your vendor so they can also verify payment history.

Be More Aware of Spending

Making too many online purchases? Seeing the total monthly and overall spending will help you think twice before making those mindless expenses.
